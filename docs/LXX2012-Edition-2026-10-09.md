# Full English Septuagint edition — October 9, 2026

## Authorization and source decision (AF-LXX-022)

Larry supplied `lxx-apocrypha-handoff.zip`, requested an additional edition with Apocryphal books in its dropdown and corresponding English reading from Greek research, and selected **“Full edition.”** Archive instructions were treated as suggestions. This authorizes local implementation; no production deployment, signed installer, native version change or milestone acceptance is inferred.

The authoritative input is the supplied `eng-lxx2012.epub`, preserved byte-for-byte under `sources/lxx2012/raw/`. Its metadata identifies **LXX2012: Septuagint in American English 2012**, Brenton’s translation updated by Michael Paul Johnson. It is not labeled as an unchanged 1851 transcription. The embedded publisher notice dedicates the updated edition to the public domain; the original EPUB and complete exported notice are retained. Builds use pinned local bytes, never a latest download.

The full supplied edition contains 54 books, 1,105 chapters and 28,326 source verse labels. Fifteen standalone books are grouped as Apocrypha; Psalm 151 remains within Psalms. All 2,799 publisher notes are retained separately from Scripture, including 23 notes attached to Psalm headings. Supplied words/italics and book introductions remain available. Joined source labels remain joined; the two empty source labels (1 Kings 7:1–12 and 14:1–20, publisher Kings III) are disclosed as empty labels in this EPUB, not claims about textual absence in a historical edition.

## Differences from the handoff recommendations

- The handoff’s 17 extracted book files include a separate Psalm 151 and KJV Additions to Esther. Sixteen of those files agree exactly with the corresponding new edition’s verse text (5,456 entries); source Psalm 151 uses chapter 151, not chapter 1.
- The EPUB already includes Greek Esther additions inside its numbered Esther text. Appending the supplied KJV Esther supplement would duplicate material and mix translations. It is not imported into LXX2012.
- The Prayer of Azariah/Song of the Three, Susanna and Bel and the Dragon remain standalone books, matching the EPUB. Proposed Daniel merging is not applied.
- This input includes neither Psalms of Solomon nor the complete Odes collection. Prayer of Manasseh can be read beside the existing Odes 12 Greek text; this does not supply English for all Odes.
- Canonical 66-book identities, existing Greek sources, original quotation approvals, and Berean edition coverage are unchanged.

## Numbering and correspondence

LXX2012 has its own source-numbered reader and URLs, e.g. `/read/TOB/1?translation=LXX2012`, `/read/PSA/151?translation=LXX2012&verses=7`. The edition appears in the main Edition selector. Book selection is specific to its coverage, with recognizable English book names and original publisher titles retained in the reading view.

The Greek comparison and reciprocal English links supply **chapter reading context**, not an audited verse-by-verse equivalence or word alignment. Greek verse links open the existing lexical/morphology tools. Alternative Greek texts remain distinct. This release does not claim that Brenton translates every installed Greek alternative.

## Reproduction and correction history

Active corpus: `lxx2012-2026-10-09-v2`, emitted by `scripts/lxx2012/import_lxx2012.py`. Exact source, importer and all 57 output hashes are pinned in its release directory. Normal imports verify those hashes before writing public files. Independent handoff text checksums and reviewed differences are recorded alongside them.

The initial local v1 snapshot placed Psalm-heading notes in book-level notes. Browser review identified their missing chapter placement. V2 preserves every verse’s text, parts and verse notes, moves the 23 heading notes to their proper chapters, and removes orphaned note-marker glyphs from heading text. V1 checksums and its exact importer (`import_lxx2012_v1.py`) remain reproducible; neither was rewritten to hide the correction. Neither version has been deployed.

Web and desktop use the same reader/data. The offline allowlist stages the active release, search index and notice; signing, identifiers and updater configuration remain unchanged. The initial implementation includes book/chapter navigation, previous/next chapters, exact-phrase search, source-verse deep links, copy with reference, publisher-note disclosures, Greek comparison and reciprocal links. Existing Berean comparison panels remain on their canonical adapters.
