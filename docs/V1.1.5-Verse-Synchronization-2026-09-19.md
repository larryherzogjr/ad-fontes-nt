# Ad Fontes NT 1.1.5 synchronized-reader release

Date: 2026-09-19
Operator: Larry Herzog Jr., publishing as Ordinary Means

## Scope

Version 1.1.5 brings the production-confirmed verse-synchronization corrections
to new signed macOS Apple Silicon and Windows 11 x64 desktop packages. When the
optional desktop-width **Sync verses** control is enabled, the pane receiving
the latest real touch, pointer, wheel or supported keyboard gesture remains the
scroll leader. Programmatic follower movement cannot take leadership. On iPad,
touch-driven movement in the compact Scripture reader is coalesced until its
momentum settles, then the taller Greek or edition-comparison pane aligns once
to the settled canonical verse. Right-pane touch scrolling and non-touch reader
scrolling remain immediate.

The three shared synchronization corrections are commits `abc9dee`, `5482cdd`
and `09cf629`. Larry confirmed the final web behavior on a physical iPad before
authorizing the native release. The desktop release increments npm, Cargo and
Tauri metadata from 1.1.4 to 1.1.5. The public Downloads page and updater
continue to serve the immutable 1.1.4 artifacts until the exact verified 1.1.5
artifacts and updater manifest are reviewed, approved and published.

No Scripture, publisher note, Ordinary Means commentary, corpus mapping,
account ownership rule, note API or database schema changes are part of this
release. Private account-backed notes remain web-only.
