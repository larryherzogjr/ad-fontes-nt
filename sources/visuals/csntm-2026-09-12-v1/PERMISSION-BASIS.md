# CSNTM visual-source permission basis

Date recorded: 2026-09-12  
Decision owner: Larry Herzog Jr.  
Scope: Ad Fontes NT public website and locally bundled macOS/Windows desktop applications

Larry supplied this project-level rights decision in the project conversation:

> Based on conversation with program and the fact that the Ad Fontes NT app is free and for educational use only, we can use any of the applicable images available at https://manuscripts.csntm.org/ .

For this release, that statement is treated as owner-reported authorization to
copy and redistribute the four applicable CSNTM manuscript-image selections in
Ad Fontes NT's free educational website and offline desktop applications. It is
not treated as permission for commercial reuse, sublicensing, removal of
credits, use outside Ad Fontes NT, or use of unrelated CSNTM images.

The preserved `evidence/csntm-terms.html` snapshot is the current public policy
context. It says manuscript-image use is permissioned individually and that
rights may remain with a holding institution. This file records Larry's rights
decision; it does not represent that Codex independently inspected private
correspondence or inferred permission merely from public web access.

The Codex Bezae source pages themselves carry a Cambridge University Library
credit and CC BY-NC 3.0 notice. All four plates retain CSNTM, holding-institution,
manuscript, shelfmark, and source-link information in the candidate registry.

This rights decision does not approve the new captions. The complete registry
and bound source files require a separate exact-hash editorial approval before
publication.
