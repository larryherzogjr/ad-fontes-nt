The preserved archive is the Codex Sinaiticus Project's official electronic

## 1 Timothy 3:16

Record `V-B47K3V16-47-1TIM`, folio marker `S-86-2v` / local folio `294b`,
starts at column 2 line 47 and continues in column 3. The variant application
for word 9 records `ος` as `main-corr` and `θεὸϲ` as a correction by hand `e`.
This supports the candidate registry's narrowly worded first-hand/correction
statement. It does not establish the reading of another witness or the autograph.

## 1 John 5:7–8

Records `V-B55K5V7-55-1JOHN` and `V-B55K5V8-55-1JOHN`, folio marker
`S-89-8r` / local folio `324`, place the passage at column 1 lines 13–18. The
sequence names the three testifying and then the Spirit, water and blood; no
Father/Word/Holy Spirit expansion occurs in these verse records. This supports
the registry's claim about this witness only.

The public verse pages saved beside this memo independently expose the same
page locators and reading text. No Codex Sinaiticus image is copied from that
project; the candidate images are the separately permissioned CSNTM facsimiles.
