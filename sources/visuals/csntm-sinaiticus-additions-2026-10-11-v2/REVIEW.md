# Sinaiticus visual review — October 11, 2026

AFNT-113 follow-on. Six proposed plates; no publication approval.

## candidate-105: Matthew 20:16

Codex Sinaiticus (GA 01/א), British Library Add MS 43725, local folio 211; Matthew 20:16 col. 2, line 12. The first-hand verse ends with the reversal of first and last; the additional saying about many called and few chosen is not in the verse record.

This single witness does not by itself establish the autograph reading, the reading of any other manuscript, or the relative weight of the alternatives.

## candidate-106: Luke 24:51

Codex Sinaiticus (GA 01/א), British Library Add MS 43725, local folio 246b; Luke 24:51 col. 4, line 13. The first-hand verse ends after Jesus parts from the disciples; corrector ca supplies the clause describing his being carried up into heaven.

This single witness does not by itself establish the autograph reading, the reading of any other manuscript, or the relative weight of the alternatives.

## candidate-107: John 1:28

Codex Sinaiticus (GA 01/א), British Library Add MS 43725, local folio 247; John 1:28 col. 3, line 38. The first hand reads βηθανια (Bethany); corrector cb2 changes the name to βηθαραβα. The correction's exact spelling is preserved rather than normalized to the displayed TR form.

This single witness does not by itself establish the autograph reading, the reading of any other manuscript, or the relative weight of the alternatives.

## candidate-108: Colossians 1:14

Codex Sinaiticus (GA 01/א), British Library Add MS 43725, local folio 285; Colossians 1:14 col. 1, line 36. The first-hand verse has redemption followed by forgiveness of sins, without an intervening phrase about his blood.

This single witness does not by itself establish the autograph reading, the reading of any other manuscript, or the relative weight of the alternatives.

## candidate-109: Hebrews 2:9

Codex Sinaiticus (GA 01/א), British Library Add MS 43725, local folio 289; Hebrews 2:9 col. 3, line 6. The first hand reads χαριτι followed by the nomen sacrum for God, corresponding to 'by the grace of God'; it does not supply the apart-from-God alternative.

This single witness does not by itself establish the autograph reading, the reading of any other manuscript, or the relative weight of the alternatives.

## candidate-110: Revelation 1:5

Codex Sinaiticus (GA 01/א), British Library Add MS 43725, local folio 325b; Revelation 1:5 col. 1, line 31. The first hand reads λυϲαντι, corresponding to 'released/freed', rather than the washing verb.

This single witness does not by itself establish the autograph reading, the reading of any other manuscript, or the relative weight of the alternatives.
