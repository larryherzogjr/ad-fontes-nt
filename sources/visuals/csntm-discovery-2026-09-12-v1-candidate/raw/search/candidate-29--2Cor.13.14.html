GA 015 Majuscule 6th Century View

Codex Coislinianus is a sixth century manuscript of Paul on parchment; 41 leaves (22 at the BNF), single column, 16 lines per column. For recent images, please visit the Bibliothèque Nationale Suppl. Grec. 1074 and Bibliothèque Nationale Coislin 202. Digital microfilm images from Mount Athos.

Location: Paris, France, Bibliothèque Nationale

Shelf Number: Suppl. Gr. 1074; Coislin 202
