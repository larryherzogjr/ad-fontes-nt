# Permission basis for the Sinaiticus supplement

Date recorded: 2026-09-12  
Decision owner: Larry Herzog Jr.  
Scope: Ad Fontes NT public website and locally bundled macOS/Windows desktop applications

Larry supplied this project-level rights decision in the project conversation:

> Based on conversation with program and the fact that the Ad Fontes NT app is free and for educational use only, we can use any of the applicable images available at https://manuscripts.csntm.org/ .

For this candidate, that statement is treated as owner-reported authorization
to copy and redistribute the 33 selected CSNTM Codex Sinaiticus page images in
Ad Fontes NT’s free educational website and offline desktop applications. It is
not treated as permission for commercial reuse, sublicensing, removal of
credits, use outside Ad Fontes NT, or unrelated CSNTM images.

CSNTM identifies the manuscript as British Library Add MS 43725 and describes
these facsimiles as coming from the J. T. and Zelma Luther Archives, A. Webb
Roberts Library, Southwestern Baptist Theological Seminary. Every proposed
plate retains those credits, the manuscript identity, shelfmark, page locator,
source link, and a project-limited reuse statement.

The public policy snapshot preserved in the approved v1 release remains the
general terms context. This file records Larry’s rights decision; it does not
represent that Codex inspected private correspondence or inferred permission
from public availability. The 39 new captions, alt texts, and evidence claims
remain unapproved until Larry approves the complete candidate hash.
